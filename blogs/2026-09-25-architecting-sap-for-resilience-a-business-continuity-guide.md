Key takeaways: Achieving SAP resilience requires moving beyond basic hardware failovers to address patching, upgrades and regional outages. IT leaders must balance Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) with operational complexity. A robust architecture covers local High Availability (HA) and regional Disaster Recovery (DR) to ensure core business continuity.
